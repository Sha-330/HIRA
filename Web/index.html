Health Index & Result Analyzer

HIRA (Health Index and Result Analyser) is a machine learning-driven health analytics platform designed to predict and analyze health conditions using patient data such as MRI, CT scans, X-rays, and blood tests.

The platform aims to improve diagnostic accuracy, reduce reliance on manual interpretations, and support clinicians with actionable insights.
